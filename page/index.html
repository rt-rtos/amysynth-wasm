SHIFT (RB)E (hold), gamepad RB; the chord buttons under the pad press SHIFT for you

Keys act while the page has focus and the console line does not. A hold is a held key or mouse button; right-click a pad button to latch it held. Tap, long press and click timing come from the simulation, counted in audio time. On a touch screen, hold with one finger and press with another; drag the knob to turn it. No gamepad.
